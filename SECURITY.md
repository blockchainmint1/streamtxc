# Security Policy

Thank you for helping keep streamTXC and its users safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not open a public GitHub issue for security problems.**

Instead, report privately by either:

- using the **🐞 bugs** link in the footer of [streamtxc.com](https://streamtxc.com) (mark it as a security issue), or
- emailing **support@honest.money** with the subject line `streamTXC security`.

Please include what you found, how to reproduce it, and what an attacker could do with it.
We'll acknowledge your report, keep you updated while we fix it, and credit you if you'd like.

## Scope

In scope: streamtxc.com and its subdomains, the code in this repository, and the way it talks to
IPFS, the TEXITcoin chain, AWS and honest.money.

Out of scope: denial-of-service and volume testing, social engineering, and third-party services
we don't control.

## Ground rules

- Only test against accounts, wallets and videos you own.
- Don't access, change or delete other people's data.
- Give us reasonable time to fix an issue before telling anyone else.
